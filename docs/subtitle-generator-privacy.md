# Privacy Policy — Subtitle Generator

Subtitle Generator transcribes files directly on the user's Android or iOS device. Media is not sent to a developer server, and there is no account, tracking, advertising or analytics.

Files selected through Files, a gallery or Photos remain on-device. On-device speech recognition produces editable subtitles. The app saves subtitle projects within its private application storage until deleted. On iOS, media selected from the Photos library may be copied temporarily into the application's cache to permit processing. The operating system may include app data in device backups depending on device configuration.

If the user requests a model download, the app contacts Hugging Face (huggingface.co) over HTTPS to retrieve licensed model weights. The hosting provider may receive standard connection information such as the requesting IP address. This model download is optional if models are bundled and is not used to upload media.

The app does not collect or sell personal information. Users can remove subtitle projects and downloaded models using app controls or uninstall the app to remove its local data.

For support or privacy requests, use the developer contact information listed in the app's official Google Play or Apple App Store listing after publication.
