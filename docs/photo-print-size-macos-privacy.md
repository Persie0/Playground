# Photo Print Size for Mac — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 3 November 2026  
**Product:** Photo Print Size (macOS)  
**Developer / controller:** Marvin Perzi, trading as Persie0, Austria  
**Contact:** persie0@protonmail.com

This notice applies specifically to the **macOS App Store version** of Photo
Print Size. It is separate from the privacy policies for other Persie0 apps.

## What the app processes

Photo Print Size is an offline-first photo and PDF layout tool. You choose
images using the macOS file picker, arrange and edit them with text and paper
settings, and can export PDFs or print through system dialogs. The app
processes the selected image bytes and document geometry **locally**. It
stores local project documents, their image assets, measurement preferences,
language preference, review prompt counters, and other layout settings in the
app's sandboxed data directory.

**The macOS build does not require an account or registration.** In the
initial free release, app billing is disabled, advertising is not integrated,
and automatic crash/error reporting and tracing are disabled. The app does
not actively transmit project images, text, PDF contents, analytics or
device identifiers to Persie0 servers or advertising providers.

If you explicitly select a file to export, the app writes it to the
location you selected. If you open a privacy, help or external link, macOS
opens your browser and that site may receive the usual request metadata.
If you choose to print, the selected printer and macOS print service handle
the document you submit. Any sharing you initiate through other apps is
controlled by those services' own policies.

The release may include dormant dependencies for store billing or crash
reporting to preserve cross-platform code, but they are not initialized
when these features are disabled. This notice must be reassessed before
enabling those services in any future Mac release.

## Permissions and access

- **User-selected files:** macOS prompts you to select images to import or
  a destination for exporting PDFs. The app does not request blanket
  access to your files, Photos library or other applications' documents.
- **Printing:** printing happens only after you request it through the
  system print dialog.
- **Network:** the initial macOS release does not use network access for
  document processing or telemetry. External websites are opened on request
  through your browser.
- **Camera, microphone, location and contacts:** not requested by this Mac app.

The app works offline for its core photo/document editing and PDF generation.

## Legal bases and retention

Local processing needed to provide the editing, saving and printing functions
you request relies on GDPR Article 6(1)(b) where applicable. Essential local
preferences are stored to provide and remember your chosen settings. The
personal information in the files you select remains under your control.

Projects and settings remain on your Mac until you delete individual projects,
remove local application data or uninstall the app. You can delete saved
documents within the app. Deleting the app may not remove files you
previously exported to other folders; delete those separately in Finder.
Persie0 does not hold a remote copy of your documents to delete.

Support emails are processed to respond to your inquiry under GDPR Article
6(1)(f), kept only as long as reasonably needed for the request or applicable
legal obligations, and are not used for unsolicited marketing.

## Sharing and third parties

No third-party analytics, advertising or purchase service receives data as
part of normal use of this macOS build. Apple operates the App Store and
handles downloads and any Store-level diagnostics under its own privacy terms.
macOS printer services, browsers and destinations you explicitly choose may
receive the content or request metadata that you direct to them.

This privacy policy is hosted through GitHub Pages or GitHub web hosting:
GitHub may process IP addresses, browser details and request logs to provide
and secure the webpage. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Your rights

Subject to the GDPR's conditions, you may request access to, correction of,
erasure of, or restriction on processing of personal data held by the
developer, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and request
data portability. You may lodge a complaint with the
[Austrian Data Protection Authority](https://www.dsb.gv.at/).

Contact **persie0@protonmail.com** for privacy questions and requests.
No online account is required to use the app, and no remote project account
exists in this Mac release.
